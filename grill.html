How to Grill the Perfect Hot Dog

ENGR 3010 Technical Writing Final Project, 2025

This is a paper written as the final project for my technical writing course. It is based on the work performed for the final project of my heat transfer course. In my analysis, I numerically solved for the heat transfer coefficient based on an arbitrary desired final temperature of the hot dog, which I was given by my Heat Transfer Professor (evidently a hot dog enjoyer). The goal of the study was to apply heat transfer principles in order to reflect the real world conditions faced when trying to barbecue the perfect hot dog on a grill, and to predict the necessary parameters in order to achieve desired cooking.
