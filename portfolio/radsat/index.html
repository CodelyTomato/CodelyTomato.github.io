As chief engineer for mission operations, I got to design the 3 week onboarding project for new members. The project I designed had new members work on a proposal for a fake NASA RFP that called for a CubeSat design that could grow a radish on-orbit. I designed a proposal and link budget template with instructions on how to complete it. It served as both an introduction to satellites generally but also key aspects of mission operations such as ConOps, budgets, and mission definitions.
