Satellites don't launch without licenses. Or rather, satellites that want to talk to Earth don't launch without licenses. I took on licensing for SCALAR in July 2026, and it quickly took over the rest of my summer. SCALAR is going to communicate over amateur frequencies, meaning frequencies need to be coordinated not only with the FCC (Federal Communications Commission), but also with the IARU (International Amateur Radio Union), all of which takes months of reading regulations, documenting, revising, and sending various emails. There is a reason that everyone I've talked to about licensing always says "get started early."

In total, I will be submitting around 10-15 different documents to the FCC, which I will link here as I finish them. Hopefully they will either be a resource for a future passerby doing their own licensing, or at least something interesting for you to take a look at and then never think about again.
