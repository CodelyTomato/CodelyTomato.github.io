This is one of my favorite projects I've gotten to work on, which is the onboarding project for new members of the mission operations team. How do you teach a freshman, brand new to satellites (and college!) everything they need to know about mission operations, all in only 3 weeks? Have them write their own mission proposal for a fake mission!

Download RadSat

In this proposal, members needed to write a mission proposal for a CubeSat that can grow 1 radish on-orbit. I had initially chosen a potato, before I did some research and realized just how big the average potato gets to be while growing. Luckily, radishes have been grown in space aboard the ISS, so there is at least some TRL towards radishes as space produce.
